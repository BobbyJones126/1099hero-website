What it does

Less paperwork. Better records.

1099 Hero helps you keep organized records all year, so tax time isn't a scramble.

Automatic mileage tracking

Just drive. Trips are detected and logged with miles, route, and start and end addresses. Swipe right for Business, left for Personal.

IRS mileage rate built in

Each business trip's deduction is figured with that tax year's IRS standard mileage rate and saved with the trip, so past years stay as they were.

Snap a receipt

Take a photo and the app reads the amount, date, and merchant right on your phone. The photo stays attached to the expense.

Expenses by category

Categories set up for your line of work, plus recurring expenses like your phone bill, so nothing slips through the cracks.

Jobs and properties

Tag trips and expenses to a job, client, or rental property and see what each one cost you.

IRS-Ready Check

Catches trips and expenses missing an amount, date, place, or business purpose, so you can fill them in before tax time.

Vehicle recalls and upkeep

Checks your vehicles for open safety recalls from NHTSA, and reminds you about oil changes and tire rotations.

Reports for tax time

Totals by category and tax schedule, plus a CSV export you can send straight to your tax professional.

A quick look

Made for a busy day

Big buttons, short forms, and nothing you have to fill in right away. Snap it now, finish the details later.

No account. No company servers. No advertisements.

Everything you log stays on your phone and backs up privately to your own iCloud account — so there's no company server for anyone to breach, and no ads, ever. We don't see your data, sell it, or track you.
