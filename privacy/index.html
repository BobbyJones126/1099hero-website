1099 Hero Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Updated: spells out when the exact start and end points of a trip are sent to Google or Apple

The short version

  • There's no account and no sign-up. Everything you log (expenses, receipt photos, trips, jobs, vehicles) is stored on your iPhone, and, if iCloud backup is on, in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers that store your data, and we can't see it.
  • Your location is used to detect and record your drives for your mileage log, and to fill in addresses for you. When you search for an address or a business, only your approximate area (about ZIP-code level) is sent, to show nearby results first. Exact points are sent in two cases. When you add a past trip "By Address", the exact start and end points of that one trip are sent to Google to calculate the driving miles (the start can be your current location if you have no Home place saved). And the start and end points of your tracked trips are sent to Apple to look up their street addresses. Your location isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't shared for tracking.
  • Receipt photos are read on your phone. They aren't uploaded to us or to an outside text-recognition service.
  • A few features send limited information to outside services to work: address and business search (Google and Apple), driving miles for "By Address" trips (Google), vehicle recall checks (the U.S. NHTSA), maps, address lookups, directions when you merge two trips, dealer search, and voice input (Apple), and subscription checks (RevenueCat). The details are below.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no data sales.
  • Subscriptions are billed through Apple's In-App Purchase system and managed with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous ID and your subscription status each time the app opens — we never see or store your payment details.

1. Who we are

1099 Hero ("the app", "we", "us") is a mileage and business-expense tracker for self-employed workers, published on the Apple App Store by Robert E Jones Jr. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. Questions? Email support@1099hero.app.

2. Information you enter

The app stores what you put into it so it can build your mileage log, expense records, and tax reports:

  • Expenses: amounts, categories, descriptions, dates, the job they belong to, and any recurring-expense schedules.
  • Receipt photos you take or attach.
  • Trips: miles, dates and times, purpose, classification (Business/Personal and trip type), vehicle, the job they belong to, start/end addresses you enter, and the driven route for GPS-tracked trips.
  • Jobs: names, descriptions, job-site addresses, start and end dates, and estimated values.
  • Vehicles: make, model, year, nickname, odometer readings, maintenance intervals, and a dealer you choose to save for it.
  • Home ZIP code (optional), used only to find dealers near you and to show nearby results first when you search for an address or a business.
  • Saved places and frequent drives (such as "Home" or "Work").
  • App settings and preferences.

All of this is stored on your device. It's included in your iCloud backup if backup is available and you're signed in to iCloud (see section 5).

3. Permissions the app asks for, and why

Location

The app uses your iPhone's location to detect when you start driving, measure the distance, and record the route for your mileage log. With "While Using the App" permission, this happens only while the app is open. If you turn on Background Tracking in Settings and choose "Always", the app can also detect and log drives in the background. That's optional, and everything else works without it. Location data is stored on your device as part of your trip records. It isn't sent to us, and it isn't used for advertising, sold, or used to track you outside of recording your drives. Some of it is sent to Apple and Google so the app can show addresses, maps, and driving miles: the start and end points of a trip, your current position when the app fills in where you are now as an address, and the area a map covers (section 4 lists exactly what goes where). The full route of a drive, meaning every point along the way, is not sent to Google or to Apple's map and address services. It stays on your phone and, if iCloud backup is on, in your own iCloud account. When you search for an address or a business, the app also uses your approximate area to show nearby results first. Your approximate area is about ZIP-code level, accurate only to within a few miles; your exact location is not sent with a search (see section 4). Adding a past trip "By Address" is different: the exact start and end points of that one trip are sent to Google to calculate the driving miles. If you have no Home place saved and you've allowed location, the start is filled in with your current location.

Camera and photos

Used to photograph receipts, or attach one from your library, as your record for the IRS. The app reads the amount and store name from the photo using Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo and its text stay on your phone.

Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

If you use voice input to speak an amount or description, the app uses Apple's speech recognition to turn it into text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process that audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. The app doesn't save the audio or send it to us. It keeps only the resulting text you choose to keep. You can turn voice input off in the app's Settings.

Notifications

Used for notices and reminders generated on your phone: "Trip started" and "Trip logged" notices, the "Still on this trip?" prompt, alerts when trip tracking has been switched off, recurring expenses that need an amount, vehicle maintenance reminders (such as oil changes and tire rotations), the end-of-year odometer reminder, safety recall alerts and recall appointment follow-ups, the monthly summary, and the Records Check reminder about entries that need details. To put the town name in a "Trip logged" notice, the end point of that trip is sent to Apple's address lookup (see section 4). We don't send marketing notifications.

Motion & Fitness (optional)

If you turn on Motion & Fitness detection in the app's Settings and allow it when iOS asks, the app reads your iPhone's motion activity (for example, whether you're in a car, walking, or biking) to tell when you're driving. That way trips start sooner, and walks or bike rides aren't logged as drives. It's read on your phone only while the app is detecting a drive. It isn't stored, isn't sent to us or anyone else, and isn't used for anything else. It's off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off anytime in the app's Settings or your iPhone's Settings.

Calendar (optional)

If you tap Add to Calendar on a recall repair appointment, the app opens Apple's own new-event screen with the appointment filled in. The event is saved to your calendar only if you tap Add. The app doesn't read your calendar.

Things that only happen when you choose

  • Exporting or sharing a report (PDF, Excel, or CSV): the file goes wherever you send it with the iOS share sheet (Mail, Files, your accountant, and so on).
  • Call, Text, and Email links: open your phone's own Phone, Messages, or Mail app with the number or address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Navigate: opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with that destination; those apps' own privacy policies apply.
  • Pick from Contacts: opens Apple's own contact picker. 1099 Hero never asks for access to your contacts and can't see your contact list. It receives only the name and street address of the one contact you tap, and fills them into the form you're on. That address is then converted to map coordinates the same way as one you type (see Apple geocoding above). Nothing else about the contact is read, and nothing is sent to us.
  • Invite a friend: sends a short message with a link to the app through the app you pick. We don't see who you invite.
  • Contact support: opens your email app. We receive only what you send us, and use it only to answer you. We won't add you to any mailing list unless you explicitly ask.

5. Where your data is stored, and iCloud backup

Your records are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. We don't operate servers that store your data.

When iCloud backup is available and you're signed in to iCloud, the app automatically copies your data and receipt photos to its private area in your own iCloud account. That lets you restore it on a new phone or after reinstalling the app. This storage belongs to you, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, counts toward your iCloud storage, and can't be accessed by us.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We don't include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools in the app.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't require an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • We don't process, receive, or store your payment information. All billing is handled by Apple.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual expenses, trips, and jobs in the app, or erase everything with Settings › Clear all data.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your phone.
  • To delete your iCloud backup, go to your iPhone's Settings › [your name] › iCloud and manage the storage used by 1099 Hero.
  • Reports you've exported are copies. Delete them wherever you saved or sent them.
  • RevenueCat keeps the anonymous subscription record described in section 4 (your anonymous ID and 1099 Hero purchase history) so your subscription keeps working and can be restored. It isn't linked to your name or email. To ask for it to be deleted, email us.

8. Your choices

  • You can change or revoke location, motion & fitness, camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition, and notification permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app. Background location and Motion & Fitness detection are optional and off unless you turn them on. The Home ZIP code is optional too.
  • You can turn off automatic trip detection and voice input in the app's Settings.
  • Because we don't hold your data on our servers, there's nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf, apart from the anonymous subscription record RevenueCat keeps (section 7). You control it on your device and in your iCloud account. If you have a question or request about your privacy, including under U.S. state privacy laws or the GDPR, email us and we'll respond.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's built-in security (your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS data protection) and, for backups, by Apple's iCloud security. Information sent to the outside services above is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Children

1099 Hero is a business tool meant for adults. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Tax information

The app organizes your records and calculates deductions using published IRS rates and rules, but it doesn't send anything to the IRS or any tax authority. You decide what to share with your tax professional.

12. Changes to this policy

1099 Hero is offered as a subscription with a free trial. Billing is handled by Apple, and the subscription is managed with RevenueCat, as described in sections 4, 6, and 7.

If the app starts handling information differently, we'll update this policy and change the effective date above before those changes take effect. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Email support@1099hero.app with any questions about this policy or your information.
